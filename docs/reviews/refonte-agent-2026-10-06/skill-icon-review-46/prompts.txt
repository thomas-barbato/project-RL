=== 81 · HL04-C03 ===
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one compact skill icon for Maîtrise des armes lourdes, a dark cyberpunk game. This is a NEW proposal, not an edit of the rejected gun.
Input image: the provided icon is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY for clear gray-steel weapon shapes, restrained dark palette and large readable game symbols. Do not copy its rifle or chevrons.
Primary request: draw a clearly recognizable futuristic HEAVY MACHINE GUN with a simple literal PERCENT SIGN "%" above it. The icon communicates a percentage bonus to heavy-weapon damage. The percent sign is the actual familiar mathematical symbol: two small solid round dots separated by one thick straight diagonal slash. One percent sign only, no numbers.
Weapon design: massive broad dark-gray steel receiver, short thick reinforced barrel pointing right, large rectangular muzzle, a chunky underslung ammunition box and one clearly coherent grip. Side-on view, slightly angled up to the right (about 15 degrees) to occupy the square. It should look like a serious futuristic heavy weapon with a broad powerful silhouette, NOT a little pistol or slim rifle. Keep the entire gun to a maximum of FIVE broad construction masses and TWO major cutout spaces. No tiny ribs, screws, rivets, cables, belt, intricate vents, gold cylinders, bronze ornaments or extra scopes.
Symbol: one bone-white "%" in the upper left area, occupying roughly a quarter of the square, clearly detached from the gun. Thick clean diagonal slash and two bold circles; immediate readability at 24 and 32 pixels. NO PLUS, cross, medical sign, arrow, chevron or upgrade badge.
Style/medium: serious grim hand-painted compact emblem, controlled broad brush texture, strong pale-steel cutting highlights, charcoal shadows, ONE restrained dark-crimson detail on the weapon. Cool gray steel rather than rusted or golden metal. Material should be worn, but not rotten, chipped into fragments or covered with grunge. Clean broad silhouettes without making it a flat vector logo; restrained believable steel shading.
Composition: square image, opaque pure BLACK background, no border/frame, gun fills about 85 percent of the width and 50 percent of the height across the lower-middle square. Percent symbol rests above the rear receiver, with enough negative space to separate it. Small clean outside margins, no cropped parts.
Avoid: ornate steampunk, sepia or gold palette, orange light, glow, fire, bullets flying out, hearts, blood drops, faces, people, hands, text labels, letters, numerals, cluttered scenery, shine or cheerful cartoon polish. No poster, comparison panel, UI or watermarks.
