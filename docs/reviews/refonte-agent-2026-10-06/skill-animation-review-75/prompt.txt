Use case: stylized-concept. Create a single production sprite atlas for a dark futuristic roguelike with illustrated comic-book characters. References 1 and 2 are the current cylindrical white blinding grenade and cylindrical blue decoy grenade; reference 3 is the current charcoal fragmentation grenade with red band. Redraw their recognizable silhouettes as MUCH SIMPLER board sprites, not inventory illustrations. One horizontal row of THREE equally sized square cells, left to right: ivory-white cylinder, muted cobalt-blue cylinder, charcoal oval fragmentation grenade with one red band. Center each upright grenade in its own cell at the same height and comparable size, with generous transparent separation. Strict three-column layout, no labels. Each sprite should look excellent when its entire visible silhouette is only about 18 pixels tall. Use strong simple dark outlines, large flat colors, at most one broad shadow plane and one broad highlight plane. A plain cap, very small simple pin ring, simple safety lever. For the oval grenade, at most four LARGE segment panels. For both cylinders, a plain uninterrupted colored body. Avoid ALL scratches, mottling, grain, photoreal texture, rivets, tiny bolts, grilles, lettering, ornate rings, micro-detail, complex lighting, glowing halos, drop shadows and background panels. This is serious industrial comic art, not cute, not chibi, not pixel art. Preserve the established white / blue / gray-red identities and basic shape distinctions. Fully transparent RGBA background, sharp clean silhouettes. Suggested atlas aspect ratio 3:1 (1536 by 512), three square cells.
