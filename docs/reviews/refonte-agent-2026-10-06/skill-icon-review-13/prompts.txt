=== D59-C03 ===
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a single compact skill icon for a dark futuristic cyberpunk roguelike, Invocation de l'Artilleur, proposal D59-C03.
Input images 1, 2 and 3 are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY: match their bold angular cut-paper shapes, thick black internal ink, warm ivory outer outline and two or three faceted flat color planes. Do not copy their subjects.
Primary request: redesign the artillery drone icon as an unmistakable hovering combat drone carrying ONE clearly mounted large cannon. Show a compact low broad angular robotic chassis in a simple three-quarter view with two short armored side plates, a small cyan circular sensor, and a large underslung horizontal gun barrel projecting to the right with an obvious dark muzzle. The gun and chassis must read as two connected masses at 32 pixels. The drone must look like a flying machine, not a humanoid face or helmet. The cannon should be the strongest distinguishing feature, but its proportions must not swallow the drone body.
Composition: one centered symbol filling about 86 percent of the square, complete silhouette with 6 percent margin, no border frame. Use broad shapes designed to survive downscaling to 32 and 24 pixels. Charcoal body, light steel plates, small cyan lens and one subdued orange accent. A small simple curved suspension/shadow mark below can imply hovering.
Background: the same plain nearly black background as the reference icons.
Constraints: no character, hands, legs, words, letters, numerals, labels or watermark; no muzzle fire, bullets, yellow stains, detailed wires, tiny greebles, complex environment or gradients. No paired guns pointing in mismatched directions. Crisp angular illustration, restrained plane count, strong outer outline, symbol not detailed scene.

=== SF00-C03 ===
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a single compact skill icon for a dark futuristic cyberpunk roguelike, Furtivité, proposal SF00-C03.
Input images 1, 2 and 3 are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY: match their bold angular cut-paper shapes, thick black internal ink, warm ivory outer outline and two or three faceted flat color planes. Do not copy their subjects.
Primary request: redesign stealth as OPTICAL CAMOUFLAGE, a futuristic agent's upper-body silhouette visibly disappearing. Show a simple helmet and broad shoulders, facing front, in dark charcoal and teal armor. The left half is solid with an ivory outline; the right half of the head AND torso breaks into three large horizontal cyan-edged displaced bands fading into black empty space. Make the camouflage transformation unmistakable, rather than just showing a portrait or a sliced mask. Keep the face a small plain dark visor, the torso/shoulders conspicuous, and the three disappearing bands big, simple and separate enough to read at 32 pixels.
Composition: centered single symbol occupying about 86 percent of the square, complete silhouette with 6 percent margin, no border frame. Large head/shoulder shape, clean black negative space between disappearing bands. Mostly dark teal and charcoal with cyan highlights and an ivory contour on the solid side.
Background: the same plain nearly black background as the reference icons.
Constraints: no medieval hood or cape, no dagger, no skull, no eye symbol, no arcane runes, letters, words, numerals, labels or watermark. No detailed anatomy, tiny pixels, dotted grid, noise, busy hologram rings, soft blur or gradients. Crisp angular graphic illustration with broad colored planes, unmistakable futuristic disappearing silhouette, designed as a 32-pixel UI symbol, not a miniature detailed scene.
