## Armes

Use case: precise-object-edit.
Asset type: palette variations of the SAME four original pixel-art gun models, not new weapons.
Input image is the exact EDIT TARGET: 6 columns x 4 rows, exactly 24 guns, each row repeats one identical weapon six times.
Change COLORS ONLY. Preserve each gun's exact silhouette, outline, pixel-art technique, size, proportions, mechanical construction, barrel, magazine, stock, grips, scratches, bolts, wear marks and placement. Do not introduce any new attachment, weapon shape, ornament or glow.
Row 1 is the same heavy industrial pistol in all six columns. Row 2 is the same assault rifle. Row 3 is the same pump shotgun. Row 4 is the same flamethrower.
In each column, recolor ALL painted armor panels and painted mechanical casings on every gun to the requested palette. Replace the original red/ochre paint, while keeping black graphite steel, bright chipped silver metal edges, black rubber grips, dark hose and tiny amber indicators intact. For the shotgun its pump and selected receiver/stock facing should carry the column palette; give it enough visible color to identify the variant. For the flamethrower the painted barrel shroud AND canister paint use the same column palette. Multiple colors on the SAME gun must belong coherently to that column. Preserve metal chips and scratched bare steel through the paint.
Column 1 RED: strong crimson painted surfaces, rose-red midtone #d7475b, deep wine shadows, pale worn coral highlights.
Column 2 BLUE: strong cobalt/electric blue painted surfaces, midtone #346bdd, deep navy shadows, pale blue highlights.
Column 3 VIOLET: bold amethyst purple painted surfaces, midtone #9951db, dark plum shadows, light lavender highlights.
Column 4 TURQUOISE: vivid teal/cyan painted surfaces, midtone #13b6b6, deep petrol shadows, pale mint highlights.
Column 5 YELLOW: vivid golden-yellow painted surfaces, midtone #e1c430, deep ochre shadows, pale cream highlights.
Column 6 PINK: assertive hot-pink/magenta painted surfaces, midtone #dd439c, deep berry shadows, pale pink highlights.
The user specifically does NOT want realism to constrain palette variety. Colors can be vivid, fanciful and expressive; violet, cyan and hot pink are intentional. Keep the rugged industrial/salvaged/military material feeling through chips, darkness, outlines and wear, not by forcing muted brown/green-only colors. No luminous rim or glowing barrel, no soft bloom or glossy clean SF surfaces.
Mandatory arrangement stays EXACT 6 columns x 4 rows; one complete gun in each cell, same row-major centers, all 24 guns. Transparent gutters, no background or shadows. Original hard black stepped outlines and crisp pixel clusters. No labels, text, icons, people, floor, blood, watermark or logos. True transparent background. This is SIX colorways per SAME object.
