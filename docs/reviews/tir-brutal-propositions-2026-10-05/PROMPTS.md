## Dix familles d'armes

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original pixel-art ranged weapon concept sheet for a rugged cyberpunk roguelike, preview before user approval.
Primary request: EXACTLY TEN very different brutal futuristic ranged weapons, arranged in a perfectly regular FOUR-COLUMN by THREE-ROW transparent grid. The last TWO cells (row 3 columns 3 and 4) are completely empty. Each other cell contains exactly one complete isolated weapon.
Established art direction: harsh industrial, scavenged and military hardware. Heavy battered steel, thick welded reinforcing plates, chipped factory paint, huge practical muzzle shapes, exposed-but-protected mechanical housings, taped grips, simple clear silhouettes. Crisp original pixel-art illustrations, black stepped outlines, hard pixel clusters and limited steel shading, no smooth vector finish. Technology conveyed through mechanical shapes rather than luminous decoration. These are ugly threatening useful tools in a grim futuristic world, not sleek modern showroom guns and not fantasy ornaments. Give each gun a genuinely different silhouette; do not repeat the same rifle with recolors.
Mandatory ROW-MAJOR order:
ROW 1 COLUMN 1: ONE-HANDED INDUSTRIAL HEAVY PISTOL. Very thick compact squared slide and upper barrel housing, blunt oversized dark steel muzzle with a small worn silver lip, scratched dark red bolted slide plate, short strongly angled black ribbed grip with sturdy heel, compact real trigger guard. Readable pistol silhouette with ample grip space for fingers. Shorter than every long gun. No rifle stock or foregrip, no revolver cylinder. This is the proposed shared drawn weapon for one-hand ranged poses.
ROW 1 COLUMN 2: ONE-HANDED SCAVENGED MACHINE PISTOL / SMALL SMG. Squat rectangular stamped-metal receiver, short perforated barrel shroud, small magazine in the angled grip, battered ochre side casing reinforced by a welded strip, one restrained old copper coupling. Stockless and compact enough to use in one hand, distinguish it clearly from the pistol by the boxy upper receiver. No long rifle proportions or second hand needed.
ROW 1 COLUMN 3: TWO-HANDED INDUSTRIAL BREACH SHOTGUN. Broad black rectangular receiver and barrel shroud, thick squared silver muzzle, worn orange/yellow blocky pump grip BELOW the barrel, robust dark rear stock and pistol grip. Oversized heavy industrial mass, pump and stock very readable, few large wear marks, no elegant hunting wood.
ROW 1 COLUMN 4: TWO-HANDED MILITARY ASSAULT RIFLE. Heavy short-to-medium armored rifle with a proper buttstock on left, angled pistol grip below rear receiver, chunky curved box magazine below middle, ribbed front handguard to support the other hand, clearly projecting thick barrel to right with squared muzzle brake. Scratched faded red bolted receiver plate, graphite steel body, pale chipped steel upper edge, tiny recessed dull amber indicator. Rugged and compact, suitable as the SINGLE shared rifle design held in front of every outfit. Clear separate rear grip and front grip for real two-handed holding. No bayonet, giant scope or excessive dangling cables.
ROW 2 COLUMN 1: TWO-HANDED MILITARY LONG RIFLE. Noticeably longest thin rigid barrel with a thick muzzle collar, large angular weathered olive receiver, compact low steel scope, heavy shoulder stock and wrapped front grip. Almost black steel with battered olive side plate. Angular brutal precision equipment, not an elegant modern sniper photograph.
ROW 2 COLUMN 2: TWO-HANDED SCAVENGED MACHINE GUN. Huge blocky dark receiver, thick perforated barrel shroud, large side-bottom box ammunition container, rigid stock and short carry handle, dented dirty red welded armor strips. A very heavy recognisable broad silhouette with only one gun barrel, no impossible rotary mechanism, no intricate dangling ammunition links.
ROW 2 COLUMN 3: TWO-HANDED INDUSTRIAL ENERGY RIFLE. Thick coil-driver silhouette with THREE broad exposed dark copper coil bands along the forward barrel, massive black armored rear capacitor box with worn ochre bracing, solid lower front handguard, rear pistol grip and shoulder stock. Slight tiny dull amber recess only, no glowing muzzle or cyan laser strips. A frightening crude factory-built electric launcher visibly different from ballistic rifles.
ROW 2 COLUMN 4: TWO-HANDED INDUSTRIAL FLAMETHROWER. Broad cylindrical burnt steel nozzle housing, thick vented front shroud with small ignition bracket, dark insulated forward grip and angled rear trigger grip, two small dented ochre fuel canisters attached directly under the receiver and one short protected hose linking them to the weapon. Rear brace/stock. Scorched faded orange factory paint, soot and welded bands. No flame, explosion, external backpack or long loose hose.
ROW 3 COLUMN 1: TWO-HANDED SCAVENGED ROCKET LAUNCHER. One enormous thick reinforced steel launch tube with a flared squared front opening, bolted dirty red tube armor, rough rear shoulder pad, two short lower grips spaced apart and a simple sight block. Tube is the dominant shape; much larger bore than guns, one tube only. No projectile, rockets outside the tube or hands.
ROW 3 COLUMN 2: TWO-HANDED MILITARY GRENADE LAUNCHER. Short wide steel barrel with thick chipped silver muzzle rim, very large single drum magazine under the middle, squared worn olive receiver, robust short stock, rear pistol grip and distinct front grip. Compact but very heavy, unmistakable drum and huge bore. No external grenades, explosion or bayonet.
Composition: each weapon in left-side profile, butt/grip towards lower-left and barrel/muzzle pointing upper-right on a modest 20 degree diagonal. Pistol clearly shorter in absolute scale than rifles; rifles are smaller than launchers. Each complete object stays in its cell, with generous transparent gutters, no clipped muzzle or grip, no overlap into adjacent cells. Clean precise silhouettes strong enough to remain readable when reduced to 32 pixels. Large pixel groups and broad light-dark masses matter more than tiny decorative detail. Maximum about 12-16 muted colors per object. Black outlines, dark steel, pale worn bevels and a small amount of old red, ochre or olive paint. No bright blue/purple luminous strips, emissive outlines, glow halos, blood, skulls, logos, licensed gun copies, text, labels, ground, wall or background shadows. Truly transparent background. Exactly ten weapons, 4x3 grid, final two cells empty.
